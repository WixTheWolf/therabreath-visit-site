From flavorfactory.net/industries/oral-care — impact and finish define the experience; there is no food matrix to hide behind.

What makes mouthwash hard

  • Full-concentration flavor on taste receptors — every note comes through
  • Cooling calibration — menthol and WS-series agents in a narrow window
  • Aftertaste duration — freshness must linger without turning soapy or metallic
  • Active compatibility — sodium chlorite and other actives need masking technology
