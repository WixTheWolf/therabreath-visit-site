Formulation principle

Built for sodium chlorite stability.

We screen every direction against sodium chlorite chemistry. Mint-forward lanes are the strongest starting points; vanilla, florals, and cucumber-water notes are workshop samples under retain review — worth tasting for direction before scale-up decisions.

× Avoid in your base

CitrusLemon-limeBerryTropical fruitPineapple

Can degrade quickly in sodium chlorite and develop pool-water off-notes within days.

✓ Built for stability

MintSpearmintHerbalGreen teaVanillaGingerEucalyptus

Preferred building blocks — but each concept still needs retain proof. See QC tags on workshop cards (cleared · TBD · concern).

Flavor trends

Fresh can stretch further — within the base.

01 · Functional + sensory pleasure

Fresh works harder when people enjoy using the product — and when flavor still tastes right on day 30.

02 · Beyond mint, within the base

Herbal, green, spice, and vanilla without citrus and berry instability.

03 · Natural-forward profiles

Cleaner cues that feel clear, bright, and intentional.

04 · Kids and adult indulgence

Room for family-friendly and more adult routines.

05 · Limited-edition energy

Limited runs create a reason to try now — seasonal LEs from the presented concept set (see flavor portfolio).
