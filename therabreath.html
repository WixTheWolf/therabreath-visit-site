How the folder is organized

Top-level structure on the share — one subfolder per active TFF product code, plus shared regulatory buckets and FL briefs at root.

MI-18727, MI-21336, CH-21205, PE-22004, MI-22008, MO-22007, BG-21425, WA-20912, ST-20913, PE-21169, MI-21168, and related codes — each holds pds, SDS, allergens, regulatory statements, and COAs.
COAs

Shared buckets
Allergens, EU Allergens, SDS, EU SDS, Kosher, GMOs, GRAS, IFRA 50th/51st, VOC-HAP, SB 312, Certificate of Naturalness, Spec Sheets, Church & Dwight Documents.
FL briefs (root)
Confidential flavor briefs by program — FL2023-02 Antiseptic, FL2023-03 Overnight, FL2023-06 Total Care, FL2024-01 Kids Bubblegum, FL2022-08 Kids, FL2025-11 Seasonals. Briefs define objectives; formula PDFs are separate and not indexed here.
C&D statements
CHD Global Flavor Statement templates (2018/2023/2025) — per-code signed copies inside each product folder.
Do not publish
Price quotes, full formula submissions, usage-level spreadsheets, EU SDS hazard ingredient workbooks, warehouse inventory, and consumer penalty analysis — internal only.

FL2025-11 · seasonal oral rinses

Brief issued July 9, 2025 by Ross Conroy & Nelly Levitsky. Three concepts for the core Fresh Breath base — mint-forward, not overly sweet, chlorite-stable. Peppermint Vanilla Swirl is the lead SKU; production target early 2026, retail Fall/Winter 2026.

Concept 1 · Peppermint Vanilla Swirl

Top-tested name in C&D concept research. Light vanilla with soft creamy mint — fresh, clean, mint-forward breath freshening.

PE-22004 Sample + preliminary qualification packet on share.

Concept 2 · Frosted Delight (F/W)

Crisp orchard fruits, warm citrus, cool berries, or spiced beverage cues — freshness must stay primary; heavy dessert profiles out of scope.

Concept 3 · Freshly Picked (S/S)

Spring/summer market fruits — bright berries, melon, sparkling citrus paired with mint. Iced/chilled beverage profiles in scope.

Qualification checklist · per submission

From DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST FOR THERABREATH ANTISEPTIC — same rhythm applies across antiseptic, total care, seasonal, and new-code folders. Check off on share; do not email formula or pricing attachments from this index.

  • Product description (pds)
  • NA SDS · EU SDS
  • Certificate of Analysis (production or sample)
  • EU allergen statement · IFRA certificate
  • GMO status statement
  • FEMA GRAS statement
  • Food allergen statement
  • Kosher certification (where applicable)
  • CHD Global Flavor Statement (2025 template on seasonal codes)
  • Free-from statement (C&D safety list)
  • Certificate of Naturalness (FDA natural flavor definition)
  • VOC-HAP submission (on share — values not repeated here)
  • SB 312 statement (usage-level docs on share only)
  • Expert or consumer data (when brief requires)
  • Pricing letter — internal / not on workshop site
