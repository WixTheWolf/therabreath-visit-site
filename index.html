  • Mint, cooling, and botanical profiles built for rinse and paste formats

Flavor trends

Fresh can stretch further.

Mint is still the anchor. The opportunity is to bring in more reasons to use the product: comfort, brightness, hydration, a little fun, and the occasional seasonal idea.

01

Functional + sensory pleasure

Fresh works harder when people enjoy using the product.

02

Beyond mint

Dessert, tropical, and calming profiles can open new doors without walking away from fresh.

03

Natural-forward profiles

Cleaner cues need flavors that feel clear, bright, and intentional.

04

Kids and adult indulgence

There is room for both family-friendly and more adult routines.

05

Limited-edition energy

Limited runs can create a reason to try now.
