Lakewood10 / 14

Formulation

Built for sodium chlorite stability

Avoid in your base

Citrus, lemon-lime, berry, and tropical fruit notes can degrade quickly and develop off-notes in sodium chlorite systems.

Built for stability

Mint, spearmint, herbal, green tea, vanilla, ginger, and eucalyptus — architected for your chemistry, then proven on retain.

Today's workshop prototypes apply this discipline. Tasting is directional — not a final product decision.
