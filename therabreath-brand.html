OXYD-8® · how it works

From How TheraBreath Works — pH-balanced stabilized sodium chlorite that generates oxygenating power in the mouth.

  • Anaerobic bacteria that cause bad breath cannot survive in an oxygen-rich environment — OXYD-8 begins working immediately on rinse.
  • Superior to unstable chlorine dioxide competitors — stabilized through a proprietary method used safely in water purification for 100+ years.
  • Full system — rinse, toothpaste, lozenges, spray, gum — attacking root cause throughout the day.
  • Clinically tested — 100% money-back guaranteed efficacy positioning.

Flavor development must mask chlorite character through rinsing and aftertaste without harsh or burning perception — core to every TFF brief for this brand.

Brand equities that shape flavor work

Non-burning

Overtly strong taste or sensation reads as harsh — out of scope for mainstream TheraBreath positioning.

Alcohol-free

Adult non-alcohol mouthrinse focus — Gen Z & Millennial growth audience.

No SLS in toothpaste

Avoids canker-sore concerns and alters sweetness perception vs. typical pastes — flavor must account for surfactant environment in paste formats.

Clean finish

Freshness must linger with a clean aftertaste — not soapy, metallic, or cloying.

Natural cooling

Long-lasting cooling technologies where used must meet natural definitions per brief.

Cruelty-free · conscious
