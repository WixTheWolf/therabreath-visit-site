Scientific hypotheses · Ryan's framing

Ross asked for the references behind these — particularly studies linking allicin and diallyl disulfide to functional benefits, and the "1 gram garlic clove" efficacy literature.

A

Allicin · antimicrobial activity

Garlic thioester chemistry — allicin formation from alliin via alliinase — literature on broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity relevant to oral flora management at controlled dose.

B

Diallyl disulfide · sustained sulfur actives

Stable garlic organosulfur compound — Ross requested references tying DADS to functional benefits; supports rationale for GA-22131 dose design vs raw garlic volatility.

C

~1 g garlic clove equivalence · dose framing

Ross noted a standard clove is 3–5 g — so 1 g per use/product is less alarming than it sounds. Many cuisines use far more raw garlic per serving. Good context for regulatory and consumer acceptability conversations.

D

Chlorite base stability · open question from Ross

"From your assessment of the sample now vs a few weeks back when you developed it — do you think this garlic character is stable in the sodium chlorite base? Is it fading, about the same?" Creator-side shelf observation is the answer Ross is waiting on.
