Our partnership

Flavor partner to a growing brand.

TheraBreath is scaling across rinses, toothpastes, and new formats. This visit is our chance to walk your team through how we develop, produce, and support the flavors behind that growth — from our Norco facility, with the same team from first sample to repeat order.

We make flavors that work inside real products — alcohol-free, process-ready, and built to repeat in production.

One team

Development, production, and quality all happen at our Norco facility. No hand-offs to third parties on your formulas.

Oral care

We understand rinse and paste formats — cooling profiles, sweetener systems, and the compliance documentation your procurement team needs.

Built to scale

A long-running partnership with room to grow. Today we focus on showing you the systems behind that — not just telling you.

A capabilities review, not a sales pitch.

Open discussion around resiliency, innovation, supply chain, and partnership vision — with real facility walk-throughs, documentation, and working sessions.

The tasting is part of the day. The bigger goal is mutual confidence: your team leaves knowing how we work, and our team leaves knowing where to invest next.

Four pillars, one conversation.

  • ResiliencyCan we protect supply as volumes grow? Quality systems, redundancy, continuity planning.
  • InnovationCan we bring ideas proactively? Flavor creation, trends, formats, co-development.
  • OperationsCan we scale cleanly? Footprint, lead times, sourcing, traceability.
  • PartnershipAre we aligned long-term? Communication, investment, shared growth vision.

Innovation & Technical Capabilities

TheraBreath built its reputation on dentist-formulated, alcohol-free freshness. We develop flavors inside that reality — including sodium chlorite stability, where many citrus and fruity profiles can degrade quickly. The 10:30 AM co-creation block puts your team at the bench with ours.

What we bring to oral care

  • Mint, cooling, and botanical profiles built for rinse and paste formats
  • Formulation inside real products — sweeteners, acids, actives, not just sample cups
  • Controlled iteration: tight development loops with clear version tracking
  • From bench sample to repeat production at the same Norco facility

Trends to discuss at the workshop

  • Functional freshness — calming botanicals, gum-health cues, whitening brightness
  • Profiles beyond classic mint — herbal, green, spice, and vanilla directions built for sodium chlorite stability
  • Seasonal and limited-edition concepts for trial and repeat purchase
  • Format stretch — rinses, toothpastes, and adjacencies TheraBreath is exploring

Live flavor creation session

Your R&D team works alongside ours in the flavor lab — adjusting cooling intensity, sweetness balance, and finish length on chlorite-stable prototypes. You react in real time; we document what is worth taking to a shelf-life-validated pilot batch.

Flavor trends

Fresh can stretch further.

Mint is still the anchor. The opportunity is to bring in more reasons to use the product — within what holds up in TheraBreath's sodium chlorite base over real shelf life.

01

Functional + sensory pleasure

Fresh works harder when people enjoy using the product — and when the flavor still tastes right on day 30.

02

Beyond mint, within the base

Herbal, green, spice, and vanilla directions can stretch the line without the citrus and berry instability sodium chlorite can create.

03

Natural-forward profiles

Cleaner cues need flavors that feel clear, bright, and intentional.

04

Kids and adult indulgence

There is room for both family-friendly and more adult routines.

05

Limited-edition energy

Limited runs can create a reason to try now.

Prototype concepts

Ten ideas built for your base.

Workshop prototypes for discussion — not finished products or launch commitments. Each is screened for stability in TheraBreath's sodium chlorite rinse base, where many citrus and fruity profiles can break down and develop off-notes over a few days.

Formulated for sodium chlorite stability

We develop inside your base chemistry — not around it. What you taste in Norco should be close to what holds up on shelf.

× Avoid in your base

CitrusLemon-limeBerryTropical fruitPineapple

These profiles can degrade quickly in sodium chlorite and develop pool-water off-notes within days.

✓ Built for stability

MintSpearmintHerbalGreen teaVanillaGingerEucalyptus

Every prototype below uses these building blocks — screened for shelf life, not just first impression.

None of these are precious. Taste, react, edit, and tell us what is worth a stability-validated pilot batch.
